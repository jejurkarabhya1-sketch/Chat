# Veyra — Brand and Product Direction

## Brand
**Name:** Veyra (working name; trademark/domain/app-store clearance required)

**Positioning:** A private, intelligent communication platform for conversations, communities, and collaboration.

**Tagline:** Closer, wherever life takes you.

**Visual language:** Deep forest green `#102A27`, lively mint `#B8F3D0`, clean off-white surfaces, generous spacing, accessible contrast, rounded but restrained surfaces, and clear status indicators. Avoid copying WhatsApp's name, logo, exact UI, or distinctive trade dress.

## Product principles
1. Private by default; collect the minimum data needed.
2. Fast and dependable on weak mobile networks.
3. Useful without forcing AI or social feeds into every screen.
4. Accessible, multilingual, and usable on low-end Android devices.
5. Transparent delivery/read states and user-controlled presence.
6. No claim of end-to-end encryption until the client protocol is integrated, audited, and tested.

## Name shortlist
- **Veyra** — preferred working name; short and flexible.
- Nexora — broad connectivity feel.
- Voxera — communication/voice feel.
- Orvian — premium technology feel.

Do not invest in public branding until legal clearance and domain/app-store checks are complete.
